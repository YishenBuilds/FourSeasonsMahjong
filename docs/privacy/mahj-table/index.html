Mahj Table Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-11-21. Developer: YishenBuilds

Mahj Table is a single-player American mah-jongg app. This page explains, in plain words, what the app does with your information.

The short version

  • We do not collect any information that identifies you.
  • There are no accounts, no ads, and no tracking.
  • Your game progress and settings stay on your device.
  • Speech recognition happens on your device. Your voice is not sent anywhere.
  • Only if you add your own key, turn on the optional AI explanations and say "Agree" in the app are the hand snapshot and your question sent from your device straight to the AI company you chose. Our own servers never receive them, because we run none. Details below.

What stays on your device

  • Your lesson progress, game progress and settings.
  • If you choose to use the optional AI explanations, your own key. It is kept in the iOS Keychain on this device only. We never receive it, copy it or store it on any server.

We run no servers that receive your data, so we have nothing to share, sell or look up.

Asking the coach by voice

When you hold the Ask button, the app uses Apple's speech recognition on your device to turn your words into text. The sound is not sent to us or to anyone else. If your device cannot do this on-device, voice asking stays off.

Optional: AI explanations with your own key

Coach explanations are free and work without any of this. If you want extra spoken-style explanations written by an AI service, you can paste in your own key from OpenAI, Anthropic (Claude) or DeepSeek.

Only after you say "Agree" in the app, each time you ask for an explanation the app sends the following straight from your device to the company you picked:

  • a snapshot of the current hand (tiles, discards, the situation at the table), and
  • your question.

Nothing that identifies you is sent. The data does not pass through us.

What each company does with it is set by that company:

  • OpenAI: its API documentation says inputs and outputs may be kept for up to 30 days to watch for misuse and are then deleted, and that API data is not used to train its models unless the customer opts in. Where the data is stored is not stated on the page we read.
  • Anthropic (Claude): its documentation says inputs and outputs are automatically deleted within 30 days, with exceptions (for example content flagged by safety systems, or legal requirements). Where the data is stored is not stated on the page we read.
  • DeepSeek: its privacy policy says it collects, processes and stores your data on servers in the People's Republic of China, and that personal data may be used to train its models (you can opt out, as described in its policy). If you are not comfortable with that, choose another company or leave this off.

These companies can change their rules. Their privacy policies are the final word, and the app links to them on the consent screen.

Charges for using your key go to your own account with that company. We do not see or control them.

Taking it back

  • Turn off "Allow sending hands" in Coach settings at any time. Nothing more is sent.
  • Choose "Delete key" in Coach settings to remove your key from this device.
  • Data a company already received is held by that company. To ask about it, contact them using their privacy policy.

Purchases

If you subscribe, the payment is handled entirely by Apple. We do not receive your card, name, email or Apple ID. The app only learns from Apple whether a subscription is active.

Children

Mahj Table is made for adults and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone, children included.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date at the top.

Mahj Table 隐私政策

生效日期：2026-11-21。开发者：YishenBuilds。英文版为正本，如有出入以英文版为准。

Mahj Table 是一款单人的美式麻将 App。下面用平实的话说明 App 怎样对待您的信息。

简单说

  • 我们不收集任何能识别您身份的信息。
  • 没有账号，没有广告，没有追踪。
  • 您的进度和设置只留在您的设备上。
  • 语音识别在设备本机完成，声音不会发到任何地方。
  • 只有您自己填入密钥、开启可选的 AI 讲解并在 App 里点“同意”后，当时的牌局快照和您的问题才会从您的设备直接发给您选的 AI 公司。我们自己的服务器收不到，我们也没有服务器。详见下文。

留在您设备上的内容

  • 课程进度、对局进度和设置。
  • 如果您选择使用可选的 AI 讲解，您自己的密钥。它只保存在本机 iOS 钥匙串里。我们不会收到、复制它，也不会把它存到任何服务器。

我们没有接收您数据的服务器，所以没有什么可分享、出售或查看的。

用语音问教练

按住“Ask”键时，App 用 Apple 的本机语音识别把您的话转成文字。声音不会发给我们或任何人。如果您的设备不支持本机识别，语音提问就不会开启。

可选：用您自己的密钥获得 AI 讲解

教练讲解本身免费，不需要这些。如果您想要 AI 服务写出的讲解，可以粘贴您自己在 OpenAI、Anthropic（Claude）或 DeepSeek 的密钥。

只有您在 App 里点了“同意”之后，每次请求讲解，App 才会从您的设备直接发给您选的公司：

  • 当前牌局的快照（牌、弃牌、牌桌情况），以及
  • 您的问题。

不会发送能识别您身份的信息。数据不经过我们。

各家公司怎样处理，由它们自己决定：

  • OpenAI：其 API 文档称，输入输出最多可保留 30 天，用于防止滥用，之后删除；除非客户主动选择加入，API 数据默认不用于训练模型。我们读到的页面没有写明存放地点。
  • Anthropic（Claude）：其文档称，输入输出会在 30 天内自动删除，有例外（如被安全系统标记的内容或法律要求）。我们读到的页面没有写明存放地点。
  • DeepSeek：据其隐私政策，数据由它在中华人民共和国境内的服务器上收集、处理和存放，个人数据可能用于训练它的模型（可按其政策中的方式选择退出）。如果您不放心，请选用其他公司，或不开启这一项。

这些公司可能修改规则，最终以它们的隐私政策为准，同意页里有链接。

使用您的密钥产生的费用记在您自己在该公司的账户上，我们看不到，也无法控制。

如何撤回

  • 随时在教练设置里关掉“允许发送牌局”，之后不会再发送。
  • 在教练设置里点“删除密钥”，即可从本机删除密钥。
  • 公司已收到的数据由该公司保管。如需询问，请按它的隐私政策联系它。

购买

如果您订阅，付款完全由 Apple 处理。我们收不到您的银行卡、姓名、邮箱或 Apple ID，只会从 Apple 得知订阅是否有效。

儿童

Mahj Table 面向成年人，不面向 13 岁以下儿童。我们不会有意收集任何人的信息，儿童也一样。

变更

政策如有变化，我们会更新顶部日期。
