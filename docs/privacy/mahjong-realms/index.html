雀境 隐私政策

生效日期：2026 年 11 月 18 日

“雀境”是一款单机四川麻将游戏：你一个人对三个电脑对手。本政策说明这款 App 如何对待你的信息。简单地说：我们不收集你的任何个人信息。

1. 我们收集什么

什么都不收集。App 没有账号、没有登录、不要求提供姓名、邮箱、手机号、位置、通讯录、照片、麦克风或摄像头权限，也不读取设备标识（广告标识符等）。

2. 联网

App 完全离线运行：游戏画面、声音和规则都包含在安装包里，App 也主动拦截所有对外的网络请求。游戏过程不会向我们或任何第三方发送数据。

3. 保存在你设备上的内容

为了让你下次继续玩，App 会在设备本地保存：界面设置（主题、背景音乐开关、音效开关、语言）、正在进行的一局的存档，以及一份用于加快显示的计算缓存。这些内容只存在你的设备上，我们无法访问；卸载 App 会一并删除。

4. 第三方、统计与广告

App 不包含任何第三方 SDK，不使用统计或分析工具，不展示广告，不做跨 App 或跨网站的跟踪。

5. 儿童隐私

由于我们不收集任何个人信息，对任何年龄的用户（包括儿童）都不收集。如果你是家长或监护人并对此有疑问，请通过下方联系方式告诉我们。

6. 政策变更

如果以后 App 的做法发生变化（例如增加联网功能），我们会先更新本页面并修改上方的生效日期，再发布新版本；重大变化会在 App 的更新说明中提示。

Mahjong Realms (雀境) Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 18, 2026

Mahjong Realms is a single-player Sichuan mahjong game: you play alone against three computer opponents. This policy explains how the app treats your information. In short: we do not collect any personal information.

1. What we collect

Nothing. The app has no accounts and no sign-in. It does not ask for your name, email address, phone number, location, contacts, photos, microphone or camera, and it does not read device identifiers such as the advertising identifier.

2. Network use

The app runs entirely offline: the graphics, sounds and rules are all inside the app package, and the app actively blocks outgoing network requests. Nothing is sent to us or to any third party while you play.

3. What stays on your device

So that you can pick up where you left off, the app stores on your device: your interface settings (theme, background music on/off, sound effects on/off, language), the save of the game in progress, and a computation cache that speeds up display. This data exists only on your device and we cannot access it. Deleting the app deletes it.

4. Third parties, analytics and ads

The app contains no third-party SDKs, uses no analytics or statistics tools, shows no advertising, and does no tracking across apps or websites.

5. Children's privacy

Because we collect no personal information, we collect none from users of any age, including children. If you are a parent or guardian and have questions, please contact us using the address below.

If the app's practices change in the future (for example, if it gains online features), we will update this page and the effective date above before releasing the new version, and note significant changes in the app's release notes.
