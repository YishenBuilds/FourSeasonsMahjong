Common questions

How do I restore my purchase?
Open the Membership screen in the app and tap "Restore Purchases".

How do I cancel my subscription?
Cancel in your Apple ID subscription settings (Settings, tap your name, Subscriptions). Cancel at least 24 hours before the period ends. Billing and refunds follow Apple's rules.

Does the coach need the internet?
No. The built-in coach explanations work offline.

Do I need my own AI key?
No. Adding your own OpenAI, Claude or DeepSeek key is optional. Without one, everything still works.

What do you do with my information?
Please read our Privacy Policy.

常见问题

怎么恢复购买？
在 App 里打开“会员”页，点“恢复购买”。

怎么取消订阅？
在 Apple ID 的订阅设置里取消（设置，点您的姓名，订阅）。请在当前周期结束前至少 24 小时取消。扣费和退款以 Apple 的规则为准。

教练讲解需要联网吗？
不需要。自带的教练讲解离线可用。

一定要有自己的 AI 密钥吗？
不需要。填入您自己的 OpenAI、Claude 或 DeepSeek 密钥是可选的，不填一切照常使用。

你们怎么处理我的信息？
请阅读我们的隐私政策。
