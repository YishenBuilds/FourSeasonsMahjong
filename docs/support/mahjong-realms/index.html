Common Questions

What kind of game is this?
Single-player Sichuan mahjong (bloody battle rules with tile exchange and void suit): you play against three computer opponents.

Does the app require internet?
No. The game runs entirely offline.

Does the app include ads or tracking?
No. The app does not include advertising SDKs, analytics SDKs, or third-party tracking SDKs, and it does not collect personal data. See the Privacy Policy.

Where is my game saved?
Settings and the game in progress are saved only on your device. Deleting the app deletes them.

常见问题

这是什么游戏？
单机四川麻将（血战到底，换三张、定缺）：你一个人对三个电脑对手。

App 是否需要联网？
不需要，游戏完全离线运行。

是否有广告或追踪？
没有广告 SDK、分析 SDK 或第三方追踪 SDK，也不收集个人信息。详见隐私政策。

进度保存在哪里？
设置和进行中的牌局只保存在你的设备上，卸载 App 会一并删除。
